# Innovations · sources

30 original drinks from historical global World’s 50 Best Bars top-50 venues, plus 4 accessible milk-clarification supplements from professional publications or bartenders. Supplements do not imply top-50 bar affiliation. Recipes are Chinese paraphrases of the linked public versions. Historical ranks are not current rankings. Flavor descriptions are editorial. Source-specific timings are preserved; absent timings are not invented.
